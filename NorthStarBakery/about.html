Our Story

Founded in 2014, North Star Bakery started as a small neighborhood kitchen with a big dream: to bring people together through the comforting warmth of freshly baked bread. Today, we remain deeply rooted in our community, welcoming commuters, families, and friends every morning.

Sourcing Philosophy

We believe great baking starts with honest ingredients. We partner exclusively with regional farmers and millers who practice sustainable agriculture. Our flour is stone-milled without chemical additives, ensuring rich flavor, superior nutrition, and support for our local agricultural economy.

Staff Highlights

  • Head Baker & Founder, Elena Vance: With over 20 years of artisan baking experience across Europe and North America, Elena oversees daily dough fermentation and new recipe development.
  • Pastry Chef, Marcus Thorne: A master of lamination, Marcus creates our legendary flaky croissants and seasonal fruit tarts with precision and creativity.
  • Front-of-House Lead, Chloe Davis: Known for her warm smile, Chloe greets every commuter and family member who walks through our doors.
